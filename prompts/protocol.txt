══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
PROTOCOL GUIDELINES
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Protocol characteristics:

TCP

• Reliable delivery

• Ordered packet transmission

• Higher communication overhead

• Best suited for stable communication links

UDP

• Lowest communication latency

• No delivery guarantee

• Suitable for time-critical communication

• Can maintain communication during certain attack conditions

QUIC

• Reliable communication over UDP

• Improved resilience

• Faster reconnection

• Performs well under fluctuating network conditions
